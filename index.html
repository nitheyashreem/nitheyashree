A little introduction

Hello, I'm Nitheyashree.

Engineering is how I learn to solve problems. Art is one of the ways I learn to see them differently.

I’m a 21-year-old engineering student who finds joy in exploring different forms of art and design. I’m drawn to the beauty of simple things and enjoy expressing ideas through creativity, observation, and hands-on experimentation.

My creative interests include mandala art, drawing, calligraphy, photography, and design. Each one gives me a different way to explore details, patterns, perspectives, and the world around me.

I see creativity as a process of curiosity, experimentation, and learning. I hope to keep discovering new possibilities in everyday and discarded materials, transforming simple resources into meaningful creative expressions.
